# Privacy Policy — Food Court Tycoon

**Effective date:** November 26, 2026

Food Court Tycoon ("the app") is developed by Nexrax. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

## Short version

- The app can send us **anonymous counts** about how the game is played and how often something goes wrong. They carry no name, account, player ID or device ID.
- In the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, the United Kingdom and Turkey, **nothing is counted unless you say yes**. Elsewhere it is on by default. Everywhere, you can switch it on or off in **Settings → Share anonymous stats**.
- If you write to us through **Send feedback**, we receive the message you typed.
- No ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- Your saved game stays on your device and, if you use it, in your own iCloud account.

## What the app sends to us

### Anonymous stats

If sharing is on, the app sends one summary for each day you play, after that day or around midnight. It contains:

- the app version and build number;
- the week you first installed the game, and how many days after installing the summary is for;
- how many times you opened the game that day, and roughly how long you played (as a range, such as "15 to 29 minutes");
- game milestones you reached for the first time that day, such as finishing the tutorial or opening a new venue;
- how many times each in-app purchase offer appeared on screen and how many you bought. This does not include prices paid, payment details or Apple's transaction identifiers.

### Error counts

The same switch covers error reports. For each kind of problem (for example, "the game could not be saved") the app sends how many times it happened in a day, with the app version, build number and major iOS version. It does not send error messages, crash details, file names or your device model.

### Feedback

If you type a message in **Send feedback** and tap Send, we receive the text, the app version and the build number. This works whether or not stats sharing is on, because you choose to send it. The message is anonymous, so we cannot reply. Please don't include your name, email address or other personal details in it.

### What is never sent

We don't collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or any other identifier for you or your device. Nothing the app sends contains an ID that links one day, report or message to another, or to you. The app decides which countries to ask first using your App Store country or your device's region setting; the country is not sent to us.

## Where it goes and how long we keep it

Data is sent over an encrypted connection to a small server we run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare handles your internet (IP) address to deliver the request and to limit abuse; we do not store or log it, or your device's browser identification.

- Daily stats are kept as totals for up to **400 days**.
- Error counts and feedback messages are kept for up to **90 days**.

We use this data only to understand how the game is played, fix problems and improve it. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

## Your choices

- **Where we ask first:** nothing is counted or stored for stats until you say yes. If you say no, we remember that and don't ask again.
- **Turning it off:** switch off **Share anonymous stats** in Settings. Counting stops and anything not yet sent is deleted from your device. Your device keeps the date the game was installed and which milestones you had reached, so they are never counted twice if you turn it back on.
- Because what we receive cannot be linked to you, we have no way to find or delete one person's data on our server. It is removed on the schedule above.
- If you are in a place whose law gives you rights over personal data, you can contact us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## What the app stores on your device and with Apple

**Local save data.** Your game progress (coins, stations, managers, research, and everything else about your food court) is saved as a file on your device. We never receive it.

**Stats waiting to be sent.** If sharing is on, the counts described above are held on your device until they are sent. Unsent stats are deleted after 45 days and unsent error counts after 7.

**iCloud sync (optional).** If you're signed in to iCloud, the app can sync your save between your own devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and is never accessible to us. To stop it, turn off iCloud for the app in your device's Settings.

**Game Center (optional).** If you sign in to Game Center, the app can submit your lifetime earnings to a leaderboard. This is handled entirely by Apple's Game Center service — we never receive your Game Center identity or any Game Center data ourselves.

**In-app purchases.** Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information. The app only knows what you've bought so it can grant the item (like a gem pack or a permanent bonus) to your save file.

**Local notifications.** The app may schedule reminders (like "Rush Hour is ready") using Apple's local notification system. These are generated and delivered entirely on your device — nothing is sent to a server to make this work.

## Children's privacy

Food Court Tycoon is rated for all ages. We do not ask anyone's age and do not collect information that identifies any player, including children. Please don't put personal details in a feedback message.

## Deleting your data on your device

- Use **Settings → Danger Zone → Reset Progress** in the app to erase your local save.
- Delete the app to remove all its data from the device.
- Manage or delete app data stored in your iCloud account from your device's Settings app under your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
