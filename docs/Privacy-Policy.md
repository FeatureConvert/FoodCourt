# Privacy Policy — Food Court Tycoon

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

Food Court Tycoon ("the app") is developed by Nexrax. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

## Short version

We don't collect anything. There are no developer-run servers behind this app, no analytics SDKs, no ad networks, and no third-party trackers of any kind. Your progress lives on your device and, if you choose to enable it, in your own iCloud account — never on a server we operate or can see.

## What the app stores, and where

**Local save data.** Your game progress (coins, stations, managers, research, and everything else about your food court) is saved as a file on your device. It never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync (below) or delete the app.

**iCloud sync (optional).** If you're signed in to iCloud, the app can sync your save between your own devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and is never accessible to us. You can turn this off in Settings.

**Game Center (optional).** If you sign in to Game Center, the app can submit your lifetime earnings to a leaderboard. This is handled entirely by Apple's Game Center service — we never receive your Game Center identity or any Game Center data ourselves.

**In-app purchases.** Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information. The app only knows what you've bought so it can grant the item (like a gem pack or a permanent bonus) to your save file.

**Local notifications.** The app may schedule reminders (like "Rush Hour is ready") using Apple's local notification system. These are generated and delivered entirely on your device — nothing is sent to a server to make this work.

## What we don't do

- We don't run any servers that receive data from the app.
- We don't use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
- We don't show ads.
- We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
- We don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any to sell or share.

## Children's privacy

Food Court Tycoon is rated for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing to gather from players of any age, including children.

## Deleting your data

Your save data lives on your device and, optionally, your personal iCloud account. To remove it:

- Use **Settings → Danger Zone → Reset Progress** in the app to erase your local save.
- Delete the app to remove its local data entirely.
- Manage or delete app data stored in your iCloud account from your device's Settings app under your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
