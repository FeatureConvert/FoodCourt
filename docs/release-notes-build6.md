# Release notes — build 6

## What's New (App Store Connect field)

**Option A — short:**

Reworked the tap combo into escalating tiers, refreshed the shop with better
value across the board, and added a way to retire extra staff for gems.
Fixed a bug where franchising during an active errand could zero out its
reward. Plus a bunch of visual polish and bug fixes.

**Option B — slightly longer:**

- Tap combo now climbs through tiers as you keep tapping (up to ×3), and only
  drops one tier at a time if you pause — no more losing the whole thing
- Shop refreshed: fewer, better-value gem packs and passes
- New: retire extra Common/Rare staff from your bench for gems (Collection tab)
- New: "Hide completed" toggle on the Achievements list
- Reward icons now scale with how much they're worth, so a big prize actually
  looks bigger
- Fixed a bug where franchising while an errand was in progress could cause it
  to pay out nothing on collection
- The rarest tool in the game (you'll know it when you find it) got a lot
  more powerful
- Various visual polish: boost button cooldowns, food art shading, HUD sizing
- Minor bug fixes

---

Pick whichever length fits App Store Connect's field. Both avoid implementation
detail (StoreKit config, exploit-prevention mechanics, etc.) — just what
changed for the player. The Gold Spatula is deliberately left as a tease
rather than named outright, matching how it's presented in-game.
