# Release notes — build 7

## What's New (App Store Connect field)

**Option A — short:**

New: Franchise Vouchers — a rare, bankable item that pays off a random
hour-long bonus when you use it. Combo can now climb higher than before if
you've invested in the right upgrades, franchising itself grants its own
permanent profit bonus, and the rarest tool in the game got another pass.
Fixed a crash and a handful of UI bugs along the way.

**Option B — slightly longer:**

- New: Franchise Vouchers — earn one every time you franchise, plus rare
  drops during play (up to 3 banked at once). Use one for a random hour-long
  bonus: a big profit multiplier, better tool-drop luck, or a boost to your
  staffed stations' output
- Combo now climbs higher than its old ceiling if you've put points into the
  right Research and Legacy upgrades — investment matters again
- Franchising now grants its own permanent profit bonus, separate from your
  star multiplier
- The rarest tool in the game (you'll know it when you find it) is a lot
  more powerful, and a lot more likely to show up once you've found it once
- Combo bar clearly shows when it's maxed out for your current upgrades
  instead of just looking stuck
- Fixed a rare crash while building a long combo
- Fixed a new venue opening scrolled to the bottom instead of the top
- Various smaller bug fixes and polish

Pick whichever length fits App Store Connect's field. Same conventions as
